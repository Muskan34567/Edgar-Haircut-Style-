What Is an Edgar Haircut?

An Edgar Haircut is a modern men's hairstyle recognized by its straight, blunt fringe across the forehead. The sides and back are usually tapered or faded, while the top keeps enough length for texture and styling.

The haircut is closely associated with Mexican and Mexican-American barber culture and later became widely popular through social media and modern barbering.

Today, the Edgar has developed into many variations, making it easier to customize for different hair textures and personal preferences.

51+ Best Edgar Haircut Trends for 2026

1. Classic Edgar Haircut

The classic Edgar keeps things simple with a sharp straight fringe, textured top, and clean faded sides. It is the foundation for many modern Edgar styles.

2. Low Taper Edgar Haircut

A low taper Edgar keeps the taper close to the ears and neckline. This creates a softer appearance while maintaining the recognizable Edgar shape.

3. Mid Taper Edgar

The mid taper provides more contrast around the sides while keeping the top structured. It is a great choice for a balanced modern appearance.

4. High Fade Edgar

A high fade Edgar creates strong contrast between the longer top and shorter sides. The result is sharp, modern, and attention-grabbing.

5. Low Fade Edgar

The low fade Edgar is more understated than a high fade. The fade stays lower on the sides, allowing more weight around the upper portion of the haircut.

6. Curly Edgar Haircut

The curly Edgar combines natural curls with the traditional Edgar fringe. The curls add volume and movement while the front keeps the haircut defined.

7. Fluffy Edgar Haircut

A fluffy Edgar uses extra volume and texture on top. This variation looks softer and more relaxed than a completely flat or heavily structured Edgar.

8. Textured Edgar Haircut

The textured Edgar adds layers and separation throughout the top. It is ideal for creating a modern and slightly messy finish.

9. Messy Edgar

The messy Edgar keeps the signature fringe but allows the top to look more natural and relaxed. It requires less precision in everyday styling.

10. Short Edgar Haircut

A short Edgar keeps the top relatively close to the head. It is a practical option for people who want the Edgar shape without too much volume.

11. Edgar With Straight Hair

Straight hair naturally emphasizes the sharp lines of an Edgar. A clean fringe and properly blended sides create a precise appearance.

12. Edgar With Wavy Hair

Wavy hair gives the Edgar more natural movement. The texture can make the top look fuller while keeping the front recognizable.

13. Edgar With Thick Hair

Thick hair provides plenty of volume for textured and fluffy Edgar variations. Layering can help control the weight on top.

14. Edgar Haircut With Beard

Pairing an Edgar with a neatly maintained beard creates a stronger overall facial frame. A taper can help connect the haircut and facial hair visually.

15. Edgar Haircut With Design

Hair designs can make an Edgar more personalized. Simple lines or patterns around the faded section can add an extra element to the style.

How to Choose the Right Edgar Haircut

Choosing the best Edgar Haircut depends on several factors, including your hair texture, preferred maintenance level, and the amount of contrast you want.

Consider Your Hair Type

Straight hair works well with precise and structured Edgar styles. Wavy and curly hair can create more natural texture and volume.

Think About the Fade

If you want a subtle appearance, consider a low taper or low fade. For stronger contrast, a mid fade or high fade can create a bolder result.

Choose the Right Top Length

Shorter tops are generally easier to maintain, while longer tops provide more opportunities for fluffy, curly, and textured styling.

How to Maintain an Edgar Haircut

An Edgar looks best when the fringe and faded sections remain clean and defined. Regular barber appointments can help maintain the shape.

For everyday styling, the product you use depends on your hair type. A lightweight matte product can work well for straight or textured hair, while curl-focused products can help preserve natural curls.

Avoid using too much product because it can make the top look heavy and reduce its natural texture.

Why Is the Edgar Haircut Still Popular in 2026?

The biggest advantage of the Edgar is its versatility. The basic structure can be customized with different fades, tapers, lengths, textures, and designs.

Current men's haircut trends also continue to favor natural texture and personalized variations rather than one identical style for everyone.

That makes the Edgar easy to adapt. Someone with straight hair can choose a sharp textured Edgar, while someone with curls can choose a fluffy or curly version.

The Edgar Haircut has evolved far beyond one specific hairstyle. In 2026, you can choose from classic, low taper, mid taper, low fade, high fade, curly, fluffy, messy, textured, and many other variations.
